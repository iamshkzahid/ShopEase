The FitTrack Pro Band is engineered for those who demand precision in their fitness tracking. With its sleek design and lightweight build, it's comfortable enough to wear 24/7, ensuring you never miss a beat of your health data.

  • Advanced Sleep Monitoring: Automatically tracks your sleep phases (Deep, Light, REM) and provides a daily sleep score to help you improve your rest quality.
  • Precision Heart Rate Sensor: Continuous heart rate tracking during workouts and throughout the day, with high/low heart rate alerts.
  • Smart Notifications: Receive calls, texts, and app notifications directly on your wrist so you can stay connected without reaching for your phone.

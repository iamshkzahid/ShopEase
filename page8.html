Product Description

Rediscover the joy of analog photography with the Instant Film Camera. Designed with a nod to the classic cameras of the 1970s, this modern iteration brings the magic of instant film to a new generation. There are no screens to check, no filters to apply in post—just raw, authentic moments captured on film.

The camera features a high-quality lens system that ensures sharp images, while the built-in automatic flash intelligently adjusts to your lighting conditions, whether you're at a dimly lit party or out in the bright sun. The rechargeable battery means you can shoot pack after pack of film without worrying about power.

  • Self-timer: Get in the frame with the built-in self-timer function.
  • Double Exposure: Create artistic, dreamlike photos by exposing a single piece of film twice.
  • Autofocus System: Specifically designed 2-lens system for sharper close-ups.
